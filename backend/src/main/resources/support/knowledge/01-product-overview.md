# mPay product overview

mPay is a customer wallet application with three core customer areas: wallet money movement, mobile recharge, and chauffeur-driven car rental.

## Support identity

The Customer Care chat is tied to the signed-in mPay customer account. Customer-specific questions must be answered using authenticated backend facts when those facts are available.

## Accuracy rule

Do not invent a product rule. When the authoritative support knowledge does not answer a question, the assistant should say that Customer Care will help rather than guessing.
