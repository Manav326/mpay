# Chauffeur-driven car rental

## Rental model

mPay's rental marketplace is for chauffeur-driven cars; it is not a self-drive rental flow.

## Payment

Customer rental bookings use the mPay wallet flow. The assistant should use the authenticated booking and payment status when discussing a specific booking.

## Booking support

For a specific booking, rely on the customer's current booking status, dates, amount, and payment state. Do not invent cancellation rules, refund timing, driver details, or vehicle availability.

## Vendor information

Do not expose private vendor bank details or other sensitive operational information to customers.
